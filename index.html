CITY FIGHTER
The game Privacy Contact

A fighting game with a city to walk around in.

Pick a fighter, learn their moves, then take on the arcade ladder or real players online. Between fights, Southbank is yours: eat at the noodle bar, get a fresh cut, play the arcade machines.

Coming soon to Google Play, for Android phones and tablets

FIGHT

  • Eight original fighters, each with their own special moves, a super and three outfits.
  • Punches, kicks, combos, throws, blocks and wound-up specials. Easy to pick up, with room to grow.
  • Fill your capacitor and call a Shadow Guardian to strike for you.
  • Five stages: the Rooftop, the Gym, the Lantern Temple, the Platform and the Docks.
  • Touch controls made for phones and tablets, plus gamepad support.

ARCADE

  • Beat seven rivals in a row, finishing with four boss rounds.
  • Win Jen, unlock the premium fighters and open the Armory.
  • Five original arcade machines: Bug Blitz, Sky Guard, Brick Storm, Lucky Sevens and Rooftop Run.
  • Every fight you win earns a free Lucky Sevens spin, with boosts for your next fight as prizes.

ONLINE

  • Open a lounge and share the code with friends. Up to 10 players.
  • Challenge anyone in the room, or watch other players' fights live.
  • Text chat and optional voice chat, with mute, block and report.
  • Take the Sky Lift to the Sky Lounge, or watch TV together on the apartment couch.

SOUTHBANK

  • Walk into the noodle bar, the barber, the tattoo shop and more.
  • Order a meal or a drink for a boost in your next fight.
  • Change your hair, beard and tattoos.
  • Talk to the locals. Their replies are written by AI.
  • Ride up to Northbend, a floating district above the city.

Good to know

  • City Fighter is free to play and supported by ads. A one-time purchase removes the ads between fights.
  • Jen, the in-game currency, is earned by playing and can also be bought. It has no real-world value and cannot be cashed out.
  • Lucky Sevens spins are free. They are earned by winning fights and cannot be bought.
  • Online play needs an internet connection. Play as a guest or sign in.
  • Not intended for children under 13.
