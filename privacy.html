Privacy policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

City Fighter ("the game") is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. Contact: kalikewness23@gmail.com.

Information kept only on your device

Your Jen balance, purchases for your next fight, tattoos, looks, settings, high scores, player name and the list of players you blocked are stored on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Online play (optional)

  • Accounts. To play online the game signs you in with Unity Authentication (Unity Technologies), as a guest or with a City Fighter ID (username and password) or Google sign-in through Unity Player Accounts. Unity creates a player ID for you. With Google sign-in, Unity receives the basic profile information Google shares (such as your email address).
  • Lounges, chat and voice. Online lounges are private rooms joined with a code. Your player name, fighter look, position, text chat and (only if you turn the microphone on) voice audio are sent through Unity's relay service to the other players in your lounge. They are not recorded or stored by the game. Text chat and names are filtered for offensive words. You can mute, block or report other players.
  • Microphone. The game asks for microphone permission only when you tap MIC. Voice is sent live to your lounge and not saved.

Talking to city characters (AI)

When you type your own question to a character in the city, the question (not your name or account) is sent to Pollinations.ai, a third-party AI text service, to write the character's answer. Don't type personal information. Tapping REPORT opens an email to us with the conversation, which you choose to send.

TV in the apartment

The in-game TV streams public-domain and openly licensed videos from the Internet Archive (archive.org), NASA (images.nasa.gov) and Google's public sample-video storage. Those services receive normal web request information (such as your IP address) when a video plays.

YouTube channels on the TV

Some TV channels show live streams from broadcasters' official YouTube channels in YouTube's own embedded player. When you watch one, YouTube (Google) provides the video and may collect information as described in the Google Privacy Policy; YouTube's Terms of Service apply.

Ads and purchases

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob can collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which gives an approximate location), how you interact with ads and the game, and diagnostic information, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later from AD PRIVACY OPTIONS in the Jen store. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

In-app purchases (Jen packs and NO ADS) are paid through Google Play; we never receive your card or payment details. Unity In-App Purchasing checks the purchase receipt from Google Play so the game can give you what you bought and restore NO ADS on a new device.

Children

City Fighter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

How long information is kept

Chat, voice and your position in a lounge are passed on live and are not stored. Your Unity player account is kept until you delete it. Information held by Google, Unity and the other services listed below is kept according to their own policies.

Deleting your account and data

Online lounge > DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT deletes your Unity player account and its data. You can also email kalikewness23@gmail.com to ask us to delete it. Data on your device is removed by uninstalling the game.

Third-party services

  • Unity Technologies (Authentication, Player Accounts, Relay / Multiplayer, In-App Purchasing): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google (Sign-in, AdMob, Google Play, YouTube): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Pollinations.ai (AI character answers)
  • Internet Archive, NASA (TV videos)

Changes
